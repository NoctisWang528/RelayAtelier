# Reference study and reconstruction limits

## Evidence actually available

- Official website: https://cromma.ai/code — Westworld tool-slot dial, tool-card metaphor, rhythm queue and woven call rows.
- Public information repository: https://github.com/CrommaAI/crommacode — documentation/media, not application source. Its license notice is saved under `research/CrommaCode-LICENSE.txt`.
- Official demo: https://github.com/CrommaAI/crommacode/blob/main/assets/game-view-demo.mp4 — local research copy and frame contact sheets under `research/`. This media is not loaded or bundled by the application.
- User's six screenshots are preserved in `research/user-reference/`: Westworld, Loom, Factory, Rhythm, Slots, Roulette. They establish the warm paper/dot field, high-key enamel machinery, thin grey outlines, soft shadows, mechanical fasteners and small pastel signals.
- Installed CrommaCode 0.3.22 was observed in Westworld and Chips; official OpenCode hookup was also observed through its UI. Existing app executable/resources were not modified. No live original animation from a successful new DeepSeek task was obtained: the authorized attempt remained silent and was cancelled.

## Applied visual structure

- Replaced the rejected dark conceptual dashboard with a floating Comic Mono mode rail, warm `#faf9f6` paper and 17-unit dot grid, enamel `#fdfcfa`, grey engraved edges and warm low-opacity shadows.
- Westworld uses a large, cropped concentric mechanism with dense minor/major degree ticks, radial bay dividers, screw heads, lamps, a fixed pointer, bus rails, glass sight chamber, flask and accumulator. Rotation uses an analytic damped spring; it is triggered by events and settles.
- Factory uses a diagonal slat conveyor and articulated arm, with a monitor/motor/turbine group. Chips uses a cropped wheel, physical feed track, card shoe, landing bed and counters. Rhythm uses perspective wires meeting an arc over a cropped wheel. Loom uses dense pastel warp wires and accumulating rows.
- Slots and Roulette supplement the original six required modes according to the additional screenshots. The tool symbols are independently drawn, not copied model brand marks.
- DataFlow uses the same paper, enamel, engraved edges and tool accents. Its circuit layout, outbound and return signals, and child-agent branches are designed for this observer.
- Landscape scenes relocate components rather than shrinking the entire portrait into a narrow center. Portrait composition remains based on the source screenshots. The user explicitly allowed changed proportions and larger available space.

## Deliberate and unresolved differences

This is independent Canvas reconstruction, not original-source reuse, not an embedded recording, and not a claim of pixel/frame identity. Original advertising screens are replaced by actual tool status; there are no rankings, account screens or sponsor assets. Tool imagery, tiny engravings, some tube curvature/material shading and exact acceleration/easing are approximations. Original numeric instrumentation is not used to imply unobserved tokens, task quality or success.

The public demo establishes observable motion categories, not exact original timing constants. The enlarged responsive composition also differs intentionally from the original small middle-pane framing. Final visual review evidence and remaining issues are tracked in `design-qa.md`.
