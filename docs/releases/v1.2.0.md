## 中文

点击底部工具栏右侧的 **设置**，即可在一个页面管理 Agent 采集、外观和帧率。设置会在重启后保留。

- **Agent 采集**列出全部 21 个来源。勾选要采集的 Agent，取消勾选即可停止采集，更改立即生效。展开“自定义会话目录”可为各个 Agent 添加目录。
- **外观**可选自动、浅色或深色。自动模式会随系统切换，已有的浅色或深色偏好会在升级时保留。
- **帧率上限**可设为 1–240 FPS，默认 60 FPS。降低上限时，后台仍会正常采集会话事件。

## English

Open **Settings** on the right of the bottom toolbar to manage agent collection, appearance and frame rate. Your choices persist across restarts.

- **Agent collection** lists all 21 sources. Check an agent to start collecting its events or uncheck it to stop. Changes take effect immediately. Expand the custom session directories section to add folders for individual agents.
- **Appearance** can follow the system or stay light or dark. Existing light and dark preferences carry over when you upgrade.
- **Frame rate** can be capped at 1–240 FPS, with a default of 60 FPS. Event collection continues at its normal pace when you lower the cap.
